## What this test found in the engine

The first run read Google: Shopping as *unexplained*. The duplicate detector tested each duplicated id's extra
amount against the gap on its own; four orders double-exported together make a gap no single one equals. The
engine now also tests all duplicated ids' extras together, and `checks.txt` prints both the per-id tests
(four, all "no") and the combined test ("MATCH"). The FP&A pack's result was unchanged by the fix (its duplicate
was one journal). Recorded in `../../../tie-out/references/matching_rules.md` under the detectors.

## The two-level test

- **Plain surface** (`tie_out_note.md` alone): one sentence per gap with the date, the order ids, and the line
  count. All three found. Pass.
- **Technical surface** (`checks.txt` alone): 837 rows read, 774 in the period, 63 outside, 4 duplicate ids
  named; every detector test with its result; the rule used per figure. All three found. Pass.

## Known limits

- The same limits as `/tie-out`'s record, plus: the click window is a rules setting, not something the engine
  can infer. A gap that is view-through attribution (no click, no UTM on the order) reads as *unexplained*, and
  the note says so rather than guessing at the window.
- Refunds are not modelled in the example. A refunded order the platform still counts reads as *unexplained*.
