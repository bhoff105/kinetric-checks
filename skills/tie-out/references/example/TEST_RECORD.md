## The two-level test

- **Plain surface** (`tie_out_note.md` alone): the three root gaps are each one sentence under "The differences,
  one at a time", with the date, the journal id, and the line count. A reader who never opens `checks.txt` finds
  all three. Pass.
- **Technical surface** (`checks.txt` alone): counts in per source (379 read, 323 in period, 56 outside, 1
  duplicate id named), every detector test with its result (nine timing tests on Revenue, the one match named),
  the rule used per figure, counts out. A reader who never opens the note finds all three and can see why the
  other candidates were rejected. Pass.

## Known limits

- Rounding-scale is a size test. A gap of the right size from another cause reads as rounding; the note says so.
- Timing tests whole dates and the before/after/window groups, not arbitrary subsets. A gap made of some rows from
  one date and some from another reads as unexplained.
- Derived expressions take `+` and `-` only. Ratios and percentages are not derived figures yet.
- CSV in; xlsx and pasted tables are shaped to `report.csv` by Claude in step 2 of the skill.
